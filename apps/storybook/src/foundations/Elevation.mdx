  <Notes>
    <p><strong>Feedback</strong> (hover, pressed, focus): color and opacity with <Tok>motion/duration/fast</Tok> + <Tok>motion/easing/standard</Tok>. Pressed shows at once and fades out on release (iOS highlight).</p>
    <p><strong>Movement</strong> (sheets, drawers, dialogs, toasts, accordion, swipe): <Tok>motion/spring/smooth</Tok>. <strong>Small controls</strong> (switch, tab and segment indicator, checkbox and radio marks, slider thumb, chevrons): <Tok>motion/spring/snappy</Tok>. A spring uses its curve with its settle time: <Tok>transition: transform var(--medo-motion-spring-smooth-duration) var(--medo-motion-spring-smooth)</Tok>.</p>
    <p><strong>Overlays</strong> grow from their trigger (<Tok>transform-origin: var(--trigger-anchor-point)</Tok>) and fade in; out: <Tok>motion/duration/fast</Tok> + <Tok>motion/easing/exit</Tok>. Use transitions (with <Tok>[data-entering]</Tok> or <Tok>@starting-style</Tok>) instead of keyframes, so an interrupted open or close reverses smoothly.</p>
    <p><strong>Reduced motion</strong> (<Tok>prefers-reduced-motion: reduce</Tok>): no sliding and no scaling – a short cross-fade with <Tok>motion/duration/fast</Tok>, so states never jump. Color fades stay.</p>
  </Notes>
