<DocSection n={1} title="Principles" meta="Voice">

<SpecTable
  head={['Principle', 'Example']}
  rows={[
    ['Short sentences, common words', '“Choose a date until 31 Dec.” – not “The selected date exceeds the permissible range.”'],
    ['Sentence case', '“Rows per page”, not “Rows Per Page”.'],
    ['No uppercase', 'Not for labels, headings or buttons – capitals are harder to read and look like shouting. Only where the content needs them: names (“med.o”, “WCAG”), abbreviations (“PDF”), initials.'],
    ['Say what happens, not how it works', '“Q3-Report.pdf deleted” – not “The delete request was successful.”'],
    ['Same thing, same word', 'Delete is always “Delete” – not “Remove” in one place and “Erase” in another.'],
    ['No blame, no jokes in errors', '“The connection was lost. Retry.” – not “Oops! You broke it.”'],
  ]}
/>

</DocSection>

<DocSection n={2} title="Buttons and links" meta="Actions">

<SpecTable
  head={['Rule', 'Do · Don’t']}
  rows={[
    ['A verb that names the result', '“Save changes”, “Send invite” · “OK”, “Yes”, “Submit”'],
    ['Name the object when it helps', '“Delete project”, “Remove Design” (a tag) · “Delete”'],
    ['Links name the target', '“View all invoices” · “Click here”, “More”'],
    ['Icon-only: the label is the action', '“Close dialog”, “Copy link” · “X”, “Icon”'],
  ]}
/>

</DocSection>

<DocSection n={3} title="Messages" meta="Errors · Empty states · Toasts">

<Notes title="An error message says what to do">
  <ul>
    <li>Name the rule and the fix: “Choose a date from 12 Oct 2026 on.”, “You can add up to 10 files. Remove one to add another.”</li>
    <li>Show it where the problem is: at the field, at the file – not only at the top of the page.</li>
    <li>Empty states say what is missing and what to do next – never just “No data”.</li>
    <li>A toast states the result: “Link copied”. Errors that need action stay until closed.</li>
  </ul>
</Notes>

</DocSection>

<DocSection n={4} title="Formats" meta="Locale">

<SpecTable

  rows={[
    ['Dates and times', 'Formatted by the locale (Intl / React Aria) – never build them by hand. Leading zeros: 09:30, 01.05.2026.'],
    ['Numbers and units', 'Intl.NumberFormat with the locale; a space before the unit: 2.4 MB, 64 %.'],
    ['Ranges', 'En dash without spaces between numbers: 81–100; with spaces between words or dates: 1 Oct – 14 Oct.'],
    ['Quotes', 'English “…”, German „…“.'],
