<DocSection n={3} title="Long words" meta="Compounds · Addresses">

<Notes title="Words that do not fit">
  <ul>
    <li>
      Components break a word that is wider than its place – a German compound, an email address, a URL – instead of widening the page (<Tok>overflow-wrap: anywhere</Tok>
      ). Do the same in your own text containers.
    </li>
    <li>
      Set <Tok>lang</Tok> on <Tok>html</Tok> (<Tok>de</Tok>, <Tok>en</Tok>): screen readers pronounce the words right, and browsers can hyphenate with{' '}
      <Tok>hyphens: auto</Tok> where you opt in.
    </li>
    <li>
      In fixed texts – labels, headings, buttons – put soft hyphens into long compounds, so they break at the right place:{' '}
      <Tok>{'Versicherten­nummer'.replace('­', '&shy;')}</Tok>.
    </li>
    <li>Better still: a shorter word. “Versichertennummer” – not “Krankenversicherungsnummer des Hauptversicherten”.</li>
  </ul>
</Notes>

</DocSection>

<DocSection n={5} title="Long forms" meta="Steps, not summaries">

<Notes title="Split long forms into steps">
  <ul>
    <li>
      More than about 8 fields, or parts that build on each other: split the form into steps (Patterns / Multi-step form) – not one long page with an error summary on
      top.
    </li>
    <li>Each step checks its fields on Continue; the errors appear at the fields, the focus on the first. Errors are never collected for the end.</li>
    <li>End with “Check your answers”: the answers per step with Change. Long answers wrap there too.</li>
  </ul>
</Notes>
