<DocSection n={3} title="Run a test" meta="About 10 minutes per component">

<List type="number" size="md">
  <ListItem>Open the component page and go to its Accessibility section – the steps are there.</ListItem>
  <ListItem>Turn on the screen reader. Do not look at the screen while you test: listen only.</ListItem>
  <ListItem>
    Follow each step and compare what you hear with “You hear”. The words may differ between screen readers and languages; what counts is the meaning: name, role, state
    and value.
  </ListItem>
  <ListItem>Note every step that does not match – and also what was confusing, too much or missing.</ListItem>
</List>

</DocSection>

<DocSection n={4} title="Report the result" meta="One issue per test">

<List type="number" size="md">
  <ListItem>
    In the table under the steps, choose <strong>Report a result</strong> for the screen reader. A GitHub issue opens with component, screen reader and steps filled in.
  </ListItem>
  <ListItem>Fill in the versions, check the steps that worked and choose the result.</ListItem>
  <ListItem>
    The result goes on the component page as “Tested with … on …”. Issues are fixed first, then the step is tested again.
  </ListItem>
</List>
