Higher contrast for “Increase contrast” in the system settings (`prefers-contrast: more`): `text/secondary` becomes neutral 700 (Light) or 300 (Dark), at least 9.4 : 1 on every surface; `border/default` becomes `border/strong`, at least 4.3 : 1. Works with `data-theme` on any element.
